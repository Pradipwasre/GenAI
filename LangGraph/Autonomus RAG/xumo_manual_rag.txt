--- PRODUCT OVERVIEW ---
The Xumo Stream Box plugs into any TV via HDMI and provides access to over 250 pre-loaded apps in one unified view. It supports streaming from Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, Prime Video, Peacock and hundreds of other services. The device offers 750+ free live channels through Xumo Play, Tubi, Pluto TV and more, covering local news, national news, sports, and kids programming. Users can maintain a personal watchlist called My List and use voice search across hundreds of apps at once.

Device models covered: Xumo Stream Box XiOne and ES1.
Manufacturer: Comcast Corporation, 1701 JFK Blvd., Philadelphia, PA 19103.

--- SAFETY INFORMATION ---
Operating temperature range: 32 to 104 degrees Fahrenheit. Do not use the device outside this range.

To avoid hard drive damage, do not move the device while it is connected to power. If you need to relocate it, stop streaming, power it off, and wait at least 60 seconds before unplugging.

If the device or power supply looks damaged, stop using it immediately. Contact support at xumo.com/support. Do not attempt self-repair as it voids the warranty.

Electric shock warning: Do not remove the device cover. Do not insert metal or flammable objects into the device. Use only the included power supply unit. Using an unapproved power supply voids the warranty.

Do not bend or damage the power cord. Do not overload power outlets or extension cords. Do not connect to a damaged or loose electrical socket.

Power supply instructions: Plug into a properly wired outlet. Keep the cord away from heat sources. Always unplug by gripping the plug, not by pulling the cord.

Device placement: Place on a hard, smooth, flat indoor surface. Keep away from extreme heat, open flames, and damp areas. Do not place liquids on top of the device. If liquid enters the device, unplug immediately and contact support.

Ventilation: Keep at least 2 inches of clearance on all sides. Do not place in enclosed spaces. Do not block ventilation openings with books, fabric, or other materials.

Cleaning: Always unplug before cleaning. Use only a soft, dry cloth. Do not use liquid cleaners or solvents.

Children and pets: Keep the device, power cord, and remote away from children and pets. Place on a stable surface to prevent falls.

Batteries: Use AAA batteries in the remote. Keep dry and away from heat. Do not mix old and new batteries. Do not mix battery types (alkaline, carbon-zinc, NiCad, NiMH). Dispose of old batteries properly.

--- REMOTE CONTROL SETUP ---
To activate the remote, remove the plastic pull tab from the battery compartment.

To replace batteries: slide off the battery cover, insert 2 AAA batteries matching the polarity markings, then slide the cover back on.

Battery safety reminders: stop using the remote if it gets warm or hot. Remove batteries during long periods of non-use. Clean the compartment if batteries leak and wash any skin that contacted battery chemicals.

Pairing the remote: The remote pairs automatically with the Xumo Stream Box if batteries are inserted within 15 minutes of first powering on the device. If automatic pairing fails, the device will prompt you to pair manually.

Resetting the remote (with Settings button): Hold the plus and back buttons until the LED turns green, then press 9, 8, 1 in order. Wait for the LED to flash red to confirm the reset is complete.

Resetting the remote (without Settings button): Hold the three-dot and back buttons for five seconds.

--- REMOTE CONTROL BUTTONS ---

Input: Changes the TV input to connected devices such as gaming consoles, media players, or speakers.
Volume: Increases or decreases the audio level.

Settings: Opens the device Settings menu.
Voice Control: Press and hold, speak a command, then release to search content or navigate using voice.
Back: Returns to the previous screen.
Add (plus button): Adds or removes content from My List.
Home: Returns to the home screen or wakes the device from standby.
Quick Access (three-dot button): Opens quick settings and search.
Play/Pause: Pauses or resumes playback.
Navigation pad: Moves the cursor up, down, left, or right on screen.
OK: Selects an item or toggles play and pause.
Apps: Launches apps using dedicated shortcut buttons.
Number pad: Enters channel numbers or PIN codes.

--- HARDWARE SETUP ---
Step 1: Connect the power cord to the back of the Xumo Stream Box.
Step 2: Connect the HDMI cable from the Xumo Stream Box to your TV.
Step 3: Plug the power supply into an electrical outlet. The device powers on automatically the first time.
Step 4: Turn on the TV and select the correct HDMI input using the Input or Source button on the TV remote.
Step 5: Follow the on-screen setup guide to connect to the internet and complete initial configuration.
Step 6: After setup, you will be directed to the home screen.

Required for setup: WiFi network name (SSID) and password, OR an Ethernet cord (sold separately).

--- INTERNET CONNECTION ---
WiFi connection steps: Go to Settings, then Network, then Network Connection. Select WiFi using password, choose your network from the list, and enter your password.

WPS connection: If your router supports WPS, select WiFi using WPS and follow on-screen instructions.

Hidden network connection: Select WiFi using password, scroll to the bottom of the network list, choose Add another network, and follow the prompts.

Ethernet connection steps: Go to Settings, then Network, then Network Connection, then Connect via Ethernet. Plug one end of the Ethernet cord into the LAN port on the Xumo Stream Box and the other end into your modem or router's LAN port.

--- HOME SCREEN NAVIGATION ---
Press the Home button on the remote to return to the home screen at any time. You can also say "Home screen" or "Home" using Voice Control while connected to the internet.

Home screen sections when online:
- Switcher: Top row showing recently watched titles and recently used inputs.
- Apps: Quick-launch row featuring Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, Peacock, Hulu, and Prime Video.
- Movies and TV shows: Trending content, genre categories, and curated collections.
- Live TV: A guide showing currently airing and upcoming programs from your selected live TV provider (defaults to Xumo Play).

--- SETTINGS ---
To open Settings: press the Settings button on the remote, scroll to the Settings tile at the bottom of the home screen, or say "Settings" using Voice Control.

Picture and sound settings:
- Picture settings: Configure display resolution using preset picture modes.
- Sound settings: Choose a preferred audio language from English, Italian, German, French, Spanish, Irish, Scots, Welsh, Punjabi, Hindi, Urdu, Portuguese, Chinese, Arabic, Bengali, or Russian. Adjust digital audio output for external devices and configure an audio delay for sync issues.

Network settings: View whether the device is online or offline, check internet connection health, set a device location, and access help for improving WiFi signal quality.

Accessibility settings:
- Audio description (AD/SAP): Enable spoken descriptions of on-screen visual action.
- Beep on audio description: Play an alert beep when an audio-described channel is tuned.
- Closed captions: Display a visual text version of the audio content.
- Customized caption style: Change font style, size, color, opacity, and caption background color.
- Highlight programs: Mark programs in the guide that include captions or audio descriptions.
- High contrast: Improve text readability with high-contrast display mode.
- Voice guidance: Have on-screen content read aloud while navigating menus.

Connected devices settings:
- Remote: Pair the remote to the device and configure it to control TV volume, power, and input. Also supports controlling external speaker systems.
- Bluetooth devices: Pair up to seven Bluetooth devices such as external speakers.

Parental controls settings:
- PINs: Create a parental control PIN and a purchase PIN. Toggle each on or off independently.

Privacy settings:
- Recent history: Review recently watched content across apps and channels.
- Allow app data to improve my experience: Control which apps can share watch history and in-app purchases with Xumo devices.
- Personalized recommendations: Enable or disable content suggestions based on watch history.
- Sale/Share/Personalized ad preferences: Manage personalized advertising and third-party data sharing.
- Learn more about privacy: Visit xumo.com/privacy.

Start up and standby settings:
- Auto standby: Set idle time before the device enters standby mode.
- Network standby mode: Enables quick network resume from standby.
- Overnight power saving: Automatically enters standby at night to conserve energy.
- Sleep timer: Schedule the device to power off after a set duration.
- Default to live TV: Automatically begin playing live TV when the device turns on.
- HDMI control: Allows the Xumo Stream Box to power on a compatible TV and switch it to the correct HDMI input.

Viewing preferences settings:
- TV provider: Displays your current live TV provider. Defaults to Xumo Play unless another provider subscription is linked.
- Set up favorite apps: Customize which apps appear in the Apps row and in what order. Up to 30 apps can be added.

System management settings:
- Restart device: Reboots the device, which may take a few minutes.
- System info: Displays device serial number, Entertainment OS version, hardware model number, remote firmware version, network MAC addresses, and UI and platform versions.
- Region and language: Set the device time zone. Set the system language to English (US) or Spanish.
- Factory reset: Restores the device to its original factory state.
- Refresh apps: Updates existing apps and adds newly available apps.
- Locally stored data: Removes third-party data including saved app logins.
